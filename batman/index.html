The Venue

The Gala

Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club

Fort Lauderdale, Florida — an oceanfront far too warm for a city this haunted, which is exactly the point.

  • The Perimeter: the Atlantic holds the eastern flank; a very calm valet holds everything else. No one gets past the guest list.
  • The Ground: real sand, real surf, no ledges to brood from — only a balcony, and a horizon that stubbornly refuses to be grim.
  • The Dress Code: formal. The mask stays home. Come as the better version of yourself and leave the theatrical one at the door.

The Best Man Holds the Line

Harvey Dent stands up for the groom — Gotham's best man, for as long as a man can stay one. He has promised a toast the whole city can believe in, and we fully intend to hold him to it.

The League of Shadows sends its regrets. The invitation reached them; they decline on principle, objecting to anything permitted to end this happily. We chose to take it as a compliment.

And you have our word, which is granite: no condemned building, no ultimatums, no ferry full of strangers left to decide our fate. There will be tablecloths. There will be a real cake. For one night, the city gets the celebration it deserves.

Alfred Will Handle the RSVPs

Master Kremer and Miss Lily are, regrettably, otherwise engaged — chiefly with one another, and with the small matter of the rest of their lives. Replies are therefore received at the manor: a pressed collar, an unhurried patience, and the quiet conviction of a man who has watched this pair grow up and is, frankly, relieved.

Some guests, sir, simply want to watch the seating chart burn. Reply early, and spare an old butler the theatre of a cold plate served to the tardy.
