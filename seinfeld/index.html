Yes, the groom is named Kremer. No, not that one. One letter off. He is a slightly more reliable Kremer. He will still, at some point, burst through the doors of the reception. Give the man his space.

What’s the deal with these two?

So you’re friends. For years. Years. And everybody’s asking, “What are you two? Are you a couple? What is this?” And you’re going, “No, no — we’re just friends.” And then one day: boom. You’re not just friends. What happened there? When did that happen? Nobody knows. That’s the whole show. I mean the wedding.

They dated, yada yada yada, now they’re getting married. …What? You yada yada’d over the best part. — No, I mentioned the beach. The beach was in there.

The reception is the thing.

The whole affair is at the Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. On the beach. The ocean, the sand, the whole production. Saturday, September 18, 2027. You clear the calendar. You show up. You have a nice time. What, you’ve got somewhere better to be?

The ceremony’s on the sand. Then the reception — the reception is where it all happens. Full schedule comes with the actual invitation. This is just the website. A website about nothing.

The registry (it’s about nothing).

Here’s the thing about the registry: there is no registry. They don’t need anything. It’s about nothing. That’s the beauty of it.

…Okay. Fine. Maybe a nice bowl. Everybody can use a bowl. But that’s it. And no re-gifting. We’ll know. We always know.

What’s the deal with the details?

Can I bring a date?

A date. Sure. Who is this person? Do we know them? What do they do? …Fine. Your invitation will say exactly who’s invited. It’s all spelled out. Read the invitation.

What should I wear?

Something nice. Beach-formal. Not a puffy shirt. Whatever you do, not the puffy shirt — trust me on this one.

Is there cake?

There’s cake. Everyone gets cake. There is no “no cake for you.” We’re not animals.

Are kids invited?

To be determined. It’s a whole negotiation. There are calls being made.

What’s the weather like?

It’s Florida. In September. It’s warm, it’s humid, you’ll sweat. Everybody sweats. It’s fine. It’s a beach.
