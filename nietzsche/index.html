Thus Spoke the Invitation

Lily is to be wed unto Kremer

Not a beginning. Not an ending. A ring — and the ring turns, and turns, and would have it no other way.

Days until the eternal recurrence begins

—and then, forever

On Marriage & the Abyss

being fragments recovered from the wedding notebooks
§ 1

The Announcement

Lily will marry Kremer.

Write it as a fact — read it as a verdict.

Of every eternity laid before them, they chose the one with each other in it. What is love but a taste that refuses to be corrected?

§ 2

Amor Fati

My formula for a great marriage: to want nothing to be other than it is.

Not to endure one's fate — to love it. And then, scandalously, to RSVP to it.

§ 3

The Heaviest Weight

What if a demon crept into your loneliest loneliness and said: “This wedding you must live once more, and innumerable times more — the same vows, the same dance, the same open bar, unto infinity”?

Would you gnash your teeth and curse him?

Or would you throw yourself down and answer — Yes. And again, Yes. And again.

What does not kill a marriage — makes it stronger. (Yes: even the seating chart.)

§ 4

The Facts, Without Ornament

Philosophy steps aside; here is the timetable.

Saturday, the 18th of September, 2027.

Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club — Fort Lauderdale, Florida, where the sea rehearses recurrence and never tires of arriving.

Come. — Even the eternal recurrence keeps a start time.

§ 5

God Is Dead — the Bar Is Not

The old idols have fallen. Let them lie.

What survives the twilight of the gods is smaller, and truer: two people who chose each other, and an open bar that will not close before its hour.

Drink as the free spirit drinks — to life, which asks no excuse.

§ 6

The Über-Spouse

You ask after the ideal partner. I will not describe the last man — comfortable, blinking, content merely to be tolerated.

I mean the one who overcomes; who says of the beloved: “Of this one I will become worthy.”

Two such now stand upon the summit. — Behold the Übermensch. Behold its spouse.

§ 7

The Will to Love

They called it the will to power. They were nearly right.

It was always the will to love — the very same fire, aimed more gently: warming a house instead of burning a world.

§ 8

Gaze Into the Registry

Gaze long into the registry — and the registry gazes also into you.

Do not flinch. Buy the heavy pot, the good linen, the absurd and beautiful thing.

The abyss keeps no grudge against a generous soul.

§ 9

Of Mercer, Who Understood

The dog has out-philosophized the philosophers.

Mercer — Lily's own — would fetch the same ball unto infinity and rejoice, unbroken, at every throw.

Here is the eternal recurrence, mastered without a single book. — Be more like the dog.
