Good to Know

A few questions

❦
When’s the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by August 7, 2027, through the RSVP tab on our website.

How do I know if I have a plus-one?

If we have reserved a plus-one for you, it will be specified on your invitation. We’ve thoughtfully planned our guest list, so we kindly ask that you only RSVP for those listed.

What time should I arrive for the ceremony?

Please arrive between 4:00 and 4:15 PM to allow time for parking, finding your seat, and settling in. Our ceremony will begin promptly at 4:30 PM.

Will the ceremony and reception be indoors?

Yes, both our ceremony and reception will take place indoors.

What is the dress code?

Black-tie optional. Dress to impress! Think tuxedos, dark suits, formal gowns, or elegant cocktail dresses. We’d love to see color, texture, and a little personality, so feel free to have fun with your look while keeping it formal.

Are there accommodations near the venue or hotel room blocks?

We’ve reserved a room block at Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club, where our ceremony and reception will take place. To book, call 1-800-524-6627 and ask for the “Kremer and Lily Wedding” room block to receive our group rate. We recommend booking early, as availability is limited. Please see the Travel tab for room rates and the booking deadline.

What’s the best airport to use?

We recommend flying into Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL), approximately a 10–15-minute taxi or rideshare trip from Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club, depending on traffic.

Where can I park?

Valet and self-parking are available at the hotel.

How do I let you know about dietary restrictions?

Please include any dietary restrictions or food allergies when you RSVP.
