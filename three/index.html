01Our Story

A friendship that became everything.

We've been friends for so long that neither of us can point to the exact moment it became something more. There was no dramatic scene, no grand gesture — just a friendship that kept quietly growing, first into best-friendship, and then, somewhere along the way, into everything.

Looking back, it feels like it was always meant to be; we were simply the last two people to figure it out. Now we get to spend a lifetime making up for the slow start — and we'd love nothing more than to begin it with you, by the ocean, on September 18, 2027.
