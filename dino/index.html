First, A Long Wait

I Have Waited A Very Long Time For This

I am old. Not old like a grandparent — old like a mountain. I have watched the land crack apart and the seas climb the shore. Whole kinds of creature came, and stomped, and were gone. And all that while, without knowing it, I think I was waiting for two small warm creatures named Lily and Kremer to find each other. They did. I am so happy I could flatten a fern.

  1. Triassic

    ~250 million suns ago

    They Were Not Yet Hatched

    I was already large. I was already stomping. Lily and Kremer were not yet even small warm things hiding in the ferns. I waited. I did not know I was waiting. But I was.

  2. Jurassic

    ~160 million suns ago

    Still Waiting. Also, A Snack.

    The land drifted. I ate a fern the size of a small hill. I thought about my mate. Back then you simply roared across the swamp and hoped. Sometimes you fought a triceratops for their affection. It was simpler. I miss it a little.

  3. Cretaceous

    ~90 million suns ago

    My Time. Very Warm. Very Loud.

    This was my swamp. Warm water, tall ferns, a sky I did not yet look at too closely. If I loved you, I headbutted you gently, or I roared until the trees shook. I am told the humans will do neither of these. I am confused. But I support them.

  4. 18th sun, 9th moon, 2027

    soon — like the meteor, but nicer

    Lily And Kremer Do The Thing

    They return to the Great Water. They will not headbutt. They will not roar (I hope they roar a little). Instead they will say human words, and it will mean forever. I do not understand forever. I have seen 66 million years and it went fast. Go to them.

Then, The Gathering

Where The Herd Will Meet

The whole herd — the humans call it a "guest list" — will gather at the Great Water. I have written down the important things below, with my tiny arms, which took a very long time. Come for the pair-bonding. Stay for the strange flat food. We will not speak of the meteor.

Finally, My Rules

Rules For The Herd (I Made These)

I am large and I have opinions. Please follow these so the gathering stays happy and no one is eaten.

  1. Do not mention the meteor. Yes. I know. It is coming. But so is the ceremony, and I choose to focus on the ceremony. We do not speak of the sky today.
  2. Do not eat the other guests. I know some of you are hungry. I am always hungry. But everyone here is FRIEND. I have decided this, and I am very large.
  3. I cannot hold the rings. My arms are tiny. They are the great tragedy of my life. Someone with proper arms must carry the little round hoops the humans trade. Why hoops? I do not know. In my day we simply headbutted the one we love.
  4. I also cannot clap. My arms will not reach one another. When you are happy, please clap for me. I will stomp. Together it will be very loud. ROAR.
  5. Mercer is in charge of the ground. Mercer is Lily's small four-legged companion. Mercer patrols the shoreline, holds full immunity, and gets first claim on any food that falls. Do not argue with Mercer.
  6. Roar when they say the forever-words. I do not understand "marriage." But I understand loving something so much you want it near you until the world ends. That, I understand deeply. So, when the moment comes: ROAR.

There Are Other Versions Of This Story

I am only one large creature, telling it one way. Somewhere in the deep rock there are other tellings. Go dig one up. I will wait here. I am very good at waiting.
