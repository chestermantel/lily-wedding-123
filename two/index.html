№ 01 · How it happened

Our Story

We were friends first — for a long, long time. The kind of friends who saved each other seats, split every dessert, and somehow always ended up at the same table. Somewhere in all those years, “friend” quietly stopped being a big enough word.

The friendship blossomed into best-friendship, and then — slowly, obviously, inevitably — into everything. No lightning bolt, no plot twist. Just the two of us finally admitting what everyone around us claims they knew all along: we were always meant to be. So now we’re throwing a party about it, and you’re invited.

Travel & Stay

The Hotel

Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club is an oceanfront resort sitting right on Fort Lauderdale beach — the ceremony, the reception, and the ocean are all just steps apart.

We’re working on a room block — booking details coming soon.

Getting There

Fly into Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL) — it’s about 15 minutes from the resort. Easy landing, quick ride, toes in the sand.
