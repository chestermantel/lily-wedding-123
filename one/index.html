Our Story

We were friends first — for so long, honestly, that neither of us can point to the exact moment it began. Looking back, it seems obvious: some things are simply always meant to be, and they don't mind taking the scenic route.

Somewhere along the way, friendship slowly blossomed into best-friendship, and best-friendship into everything. We'd tell you the rest of the story, but we'd much rather show you — on the beach in Fort Lauderdale, September 18, 2027. We can't wait to see you there.

Travel & Stay

The Hotel

Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club is an oceanfront resort right on Fort Lauderdale beach — the ceremony, the reception, and the ocean, all in one lovely place.

We're working on a room block — booking details coming soon.

Getting There

Fly into Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL) — it's about 15 minutes from the resort.

Rideshares and taxis are easy to find at the airport, and the drive to the beach is a short one.

Things to Do

Making a weekend of it? A few favorites nearby:

  • Fort Lauderdale Beach
  • Las Olas Boulevard — shopping & restaurants
  • The Water Taxi — along the Intracoastal
  • Bonnet House Museum & Gardens
  • Hugh Taylor Birch State Park
  • Everglades airboat tours
