PreambleOf the Recital of Purpose

The High Contracting Parties, Lily of the First Part and Kremer of the Second Part, mindful that two sovereign temperaments long orbiting the same table are best served not by conquest but by linkage, and persuaded that no lasting stability is achieved through vague sentiment alone, do hereby resolve to convert an affectionate status quo into a durable, and it is hoped permanent, peace.

Whereas the parties have, over successive rounds of negotiation conducted in kitchens, airports, and the low-intensity theatre of the shared calendar, established mutual confidence; and whereas each recognizes in the other a rival power whose interests are, on balance, better advanced in coalition than in opposition — the parties proceed to accord.

On 18 September 2027, at the Lago Mar Beach Resort & Club, the parties will enter into accord. The instrument shall be executed oceanside, before assembled witnesses of both delegations, and shall take effect at the exchange of the operative sentence.

Article ITerms of the Accord

The following provisions are agreed in good faith and in full expectation of reciprocal compliance.

  1. On the division of the household — a matter of careful negotiation. Spheres of influence are recognized: the kitchen, the thermostat, and the correct loading of the dishwasher shall be administered by mutual consultation, with escalation to summit only in extremis.
  2. On linkage — no grievance shall be raised in isolation. All matters are connected; a concession in one theatre may be redeemed in another, and the ledger is settled not nightly but over the long arc of the alliance.
  3. On the remote control — sovereignty is shared and rotating. Neither party shall claim exclusive dominion, and the annexation of the last slice shall require prior notification.
  4. On non-aggression — disputes shall be resolved by dialogue before midnight; no party shall retire to a separate frontier while a communiqué remains unsigned.
  5. On Mercer — the standing envoy of the dog delegation, and a plenipotentiary of Lily's household, shall receive full diplomatic recognition, unlimited access to the sofa, and most-favored-nation status at every meal.
“The parties affirm that the object of the alliance is not the abolition of difference, but its orderly management — and that peace, properly constructed, outlasts the passions that founded it.” — from the operative text of the accord

Marginal note, in the Secretary's hand: “A détente is not the absence of tension. It is the discovery that one prefers this particular tension to any available peace.”

Neither party seeks hegemony. Each concedes that the other holds levers it cannot itself replace, and both accept that the alliance, like all durable arrangements, is maintained less by treaty than by the daily, unglamorous labor of keeping faith. The balance is not frozen; it is tended.
